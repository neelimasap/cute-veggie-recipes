Veggie Onigiri おにぎり

Rice triangles wearing little seaweed jackets! 🍙

⏰ 15 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 cups cooked short-grain rice (warm)
  • 1 pinch salt
  • Nori (seaweed) strips
  • Fillings: corn + mayo, pickled plum (umeboshi), or sesame seeds
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Wash your hands and wet them with a little water.
  2. Sprinkle a tiny bit of salt on your palms.
  3. Scoop some rice and make a small dent in the middle.
  4. Pop in your filling and cover it with more rice.
  5. Gently press into a triangle shape with cupped hands.
  6. Wrap a nori strip around the bottom — done!

Tofu Miso Soup 味噌汁

A cozy, warm hug in a bowl 🥣

⏰ 20 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 4 cups veggie dashi (kombu + dried shiitake soaked in water)
  • 3 tbsp miso paste
  • 1/2 block soft tofu, cubed
  • 1 tbsp dried wakame seaweed
  • 2 green onions, sliced
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. (Grown-up) Soak kombu and shiitake in water 30 min, then warm gently — that's your veggie dashi!
  2. Remove the kombu and bring the broth to a gentle simmer.
  3. Add tofu cubes and wakame; cook 2 minutes.
  4. Turn off heat. Stir miso into a ladle of broth until smooth, then pour it back in.
  5. Top with green onions and slurp happily!

Veggie Gyoza 野菜餃子

Crispy-bottomed dumplings shaped like little purses 🥟

⏰ 45 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 24 round gyoza wrappers
  • 2 cups finely chopped cabbage
  • 4 shiitake mushrooms, chopped
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1/2 block firm tofu, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Oil and water for cooking
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Mix cabbage, mushrooms, carrot, tofu, soy sauce and sesame oil in a bowl.
  2. Put a spoonful of filling in the middle of a wrapper.
  3. Wet the edge with water, fold in half and pinch little pleats.
  4. (Grown-up) Fry gyoza in an oiled pan until the bottoms are golden.
  5. (Grown-up) Add 1/4 cup water, cover and steam 3–4 minutes.
  6. Dip in soy sauce + a splash of rice vinegar. Yum!

Yaki Udon 焼きうどん

Thick, slurpy stir-fried noodles 🍜

⏰ 20 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 packs udon noodles
  • 1 cup cabbage, sliced
  • 1 carrot, thin sticks
  • 1 cup mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetarian 'oyster' (mushroom) sauce or 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp oil
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Loosen the udon in warm water and drain.
  2. (Grown-up) Heat oil in a big pan and stir-fry the veggies 3 minutes.
  3. Add the noodles and the sauces.
  4. Toss everything together for 2 minutes until shiny.
  5. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve!

Tamagoyaki 卵焼き

A fluffy, sweet rolled omelette — like a sunny sponge! 🍳

⏰ 15 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp water (or kombu dashi)
  • A little oil
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Whisk eggs, sugar, soy sauce and water together.
  2. (Grown-up) Heat a lightly oiled pan on low.
  3. Pour in a thin layer of egg; when almost set, roll it to one side.
  4. Pour another thin layer, lifting the roll so egg flows under it.
  5. Roll again! Repeat until the egg is used up.
  6. Let it cool a bit, then slice into cute pieces.

Veggie Bibimbap 비빔밥

A rainbow bowl you mix all together! 🌈

⏰ 35 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 1 carrot, thin sticks
  • 1 zucchini, thin sticks
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 1 cup bean sprouts
  • 4 shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 2 eggs (or tofu)
  • Gochujang (mild amount), sesame oil, sesame seeds
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Cook each veggie separately with a drop of sesame oil and a pinch of salt.
  2. (Grown-up) Fry the eggs sunny-side up (or pan-fry tofu).
  3. Put rice in bowls.
  4. Arrange the veggies around the top like a rainbow 🌈.
  5. Add the egg in the middle and a little gochujang.
  6. Mix, mix, mix — then eat!

Veggie Kimbap 김밥

Rolled rice with a colorful surprise inside 🌀

⏰ 40 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 4 nori sheets
  • 3 cups cooked rice + 1 tbsp sesame oil + pinch of salt
  • 1 carrot, sticks (sautéed)
  • Spinach, blanched
  • Yellow pickled radish (danmuji)
  • Egg strips or baked tofu strips
  • Cucumber sticks
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Mix the rice with sesame oil and salt.
  2. Lay nori shiny-side down on a bamboo mat.
  3. Spread rice thinly over 2/3 of the nori.
  4. Line up the fillings in rows across the rice.
  5. Roll it up tightly, using the mat to help.
  6. (Grown-up) Brush with sesame oil and slice into rounds.

Japchae 잡채

Glassy sweet-potato noodles with veggies ✨

⏰ 35 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 200 g sweet potato glass noodles (dangmyeon)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 carrot, sticks
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 5 shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp sesame oil
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. (Grown-up) Boil noodles 6 minutes, drain and rinse.
  2. Snip the noodles a few times with kitchen scissors.
  3. Stir-fry onion, carrot and mushrooms until soft.
  4. Add spinach until it wilts.
  5. Toss in the noodles with soy sauce, sugar and sesame oil.
  6. Sprinkle sesame seeds — sparkly noodles ready!

Mild Tteokbokki 떡볶이

Chewy rice cakes in a sweet, red sauce 🍡

⏰ 25 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 300 g rice cakes (tteok)
  • 2 cups water with 1 piece kombu (veggie stock)
  • 1 tbsp gochujang (or less for mild!)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1–2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 green onion, 1 cup cabbage
  • Optional: boiled eggs, cheese on top
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Soak the rice cakes in warm water 10 minutes if hard.
  2. (Grown-up) Simmer kombu in water 5 minutes, then remove it.
  3. Stir in gochujang, soy sauce and sugar.
  4. Add rice cakes and cabbage; simmer 8–10 minutes until saucy.
  5. Add green onions (and eggs or cheese!) and serve.
🇰🇷 Korean

Hotteok 호떡

Sweet pancakes with a gooey brown-sugar center 🥞

⏰ 1 hr 30 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast, 1 tbsp sugar, 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup warm milk
  • Filling: 1/2 cup brown sugar, 1 tsp cinnamon, chopped nuts or seeds
  • Oil for the pan
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, salt and warm milk into a sticky dough.
  2. Cover and let it rise for 1 hour until puffy.
  3. Mix the brown sugar, cinnamon and nuts for the filling.
  4. With oiled hands, flatten a dough ball, add filling and pinch closed.
  5. (Grown-up) Fry in an oiled pan, pressing flat with a spatula, 2–3 min per side.
  6. Careful — the filling is HOT! Let it cool a little first.

🌱 Every recipe is vegetarian (kombu & shiitake broths, no fish sauce or bonito). Eggs & milk appear in some recipes.
