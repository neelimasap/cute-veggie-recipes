Veggie Onigiri おにぎり

Rice triangles in crisp seaweed jackets — the ultimate snack 🍙

⏰ 15 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 cups cooked short-grain rice (warm)
  • 1 pinch salt
  • Nori (seaweed) strips
  • Fillings: corn + mayo, pickled plum (umeboshi), or sesame seeds
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Wash your hands, then wet them with a little water so the rice won’t stick.
  2. Sprinkle a tiny bit of salt on your palms.
  3. Scoop some rice and make a small dent in the middle.
  4. Pop in your filling and cover it with more rice.
  5. Gently press into a triangle shape with cupped hands.
  6. Wrap a nori strip around the bottom — done!

Tofu Miso Soup 味噌汁

Cozy, savory, and ready in 20 minutes 🥣

⏰ 20 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 4 cups veggie dashi (kombu + dried shiitake soaked in water)
  • 3 tbsp miso paste
  • 1/2 block soft tofu, cubed
  • 1 tbsp dried wakame seaweed
  • 2 green onions, sliced
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveSoak kombu and shiitake in water 30 min, then warm gently — that's your veggie dashi!
  2. Remove the kombu and bring the broth to a gentle simmer.
  3. Add tofu cubes and wakame; cook 2 minutes.
  4. Turn off heat. Stir miso into a ladle of broth until smooth, then pour it back in.
  5. Top with green onions and enjoy!

Veggie Gyoza 野菜餃子

Crispy-bottomed dumplings shaped like little purses 🥟

⏰ 45 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 24 round gyoza wrappers
  • 2 cups finely chopped cabbage
  • 4 shiitake mushrooms, chopped
  • 1 carrot, grated
  • 1/2 block firm tofu, crumbled
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Oil and water for cooking
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Mix cabbage, mushrooms, carrot, tofu, soy sauce and sesame oil in a bowl.
  2. Put a spoonful of filling in the middle of a wrapper.
  3. Wet the edge with water, fold in half and pinch little pleats.
  4. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveFry gyoza in an oiled pan until the bottoms are golden.
  5. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveAdd 1/4 cup water, cover and steam 3–4 minutes.
  6. Dip in soy sauce with a splash of rice vinegar.

Yaki Udon 焼きうどん

Thick, slurpy stir-fried noodles 🍜

⏰ 20 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 packs udon noodles
  • 1 cup cabbage, sliced
  • 1 carrot, thin sticks
  • 1 cup mushrooms
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp vegetarian 'oyster' (mushroom) sauce or 1 tsp sugar
  • 1 tbsp oil
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Loosen the udon in warm water and drain.
  2. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveHeat oil in a big pan and stir-fry the veggies 3 minutes.
  3. Add the noodles and the sauces.
  4. Toss everything together for 2 minutes until shiny.
  5. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve!

Tamagoyaki 卵焼き

A fluffy, slightly sweet rolled omelette — a bento classic 🍳

⏰ 15 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian🥚 Egg
🛒 Ingredients
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp water (or kombu dashi)
  • A little oil
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Whisk eggs, sugar, soy sauce and water together.
  2. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveHeat a lightly oiled pan on low.
  3. Pour in a thin layer of egg; when almost set, roll it to one side.
  4. Pour another thin layer, lifting the roll so egg flows under it.
  5. Roll again! Repeat until the egg is used up.
  6. Let it cool a bit, then slice into cute pieces.

Veggie Tempura 天ぷら

Light, golden veggie bites with the best crunch 🍠

⏰ 30 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 1 sweet potato, sliced into thin rounds
  • 1 small zucchini or 6 green beans
  • 4 shiitake mushrooms
  • 1 cup cold flour (or tempura flour)
  • 1 cup ice-cold sparkling water
  • Oil for frying
  • Dip: 3 tbsp soy sauce + 3 tbsp kombu-shiitake dashi + 1 tsp sugar
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Wash the veggies and pat them dry — dry veggies get crunchier!
  2. Stir flour and ice-cold water together just a little (lumps are OK!).
  3. 🔥 Adult help: hot oilHeat oil in a deep pot to about 340°F / 170°C.
  4. Dip a veggie slice into the batter.
  5. 🔥 Adult help: hot oilGently lower it into the oil and fry 2–3 minutes until light golden.
  6. 🔥 Adult help: hot oilLift out onto paper towels, then dip in the sauce and crunch!

Inari Sushi いなり寿司

Sweet tofu pockets stuffed with seasoned rice 🦊

⏰ 20 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 8–10 seasoned inari age pockets (sweet fried tofu, from a can or pack — check the label for bonito/dashi; pick a vegetarian brand)
  • 2 cups warm cooked short-grain rice
  • 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, 1/2 tsp salt
  • Toasted sesame seeds
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Mix vinegar, sugar and salt, then fold it into the warm rice.
  2. Stir in a sprinkle of sesame seeds.
  3. Gently squeeze extra juice from each tofu pocket.
  4. Open the pocket carefully like a tiny bag.
  5. Stuff it with a small ball of rice and tuck the edges in.
  6. Line them up on a plate — fox-ear style 🦊

Cucumber Sunomono 酢の物

A cool, tangy cucumber salad with wiggly seaweed 🥒

⏰ 15 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 Japanese or mini cucumbers
  • 1 tbsp dried wakame seaweed
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp soy sauce
  • Sesame seeds
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Soak the wakame in water for 5 minutes — it grows a lot!
  2. 🔪 Adult help: knifeSlice the cucumbers very thin.
  3. Sprinkle the cucumbers with salt, wait 5 minutes, then squeeze out the water.
  4. Mix vinegar, sugar and soy sauce in a bowl until the sugar melts.
  5. Add the cucumbers and drained wakame and toss.
  6. Top with sesame seeds and serve cold!

Kappa Maki かっぱ巻き

Skinny cucumber rolls — crisp, fresh and super snackable 🥒

⏰ 40 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 cups short-grain (sushi) rice + 2 cups water
  • Sushi vinegar: 3 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp salt
  • 2 nori sheets, cut in half
  • 1 Japanese cucumber
  • Toasted sesame seeds
  • Soy sauce for dipping
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveRinse the rice until the water is clear, then cook it (rice cooker or pot).
  2. Stir sugar and salt into the vinegar, then gently fold it into the warm rice. Let it cool.
  3. 🔪 Adult help: knifeCut the cucumber into long, thin sticks.
  4. Lay a half nori sheet on a bamboo mat, spread a thin layer of rice, leaving a strip bare at the top.
  5. Add a cucumber stick and sesame seeds, then roll up tightly with the mat.
  6. 🔪 Adult help: knifeSlice each roll into 6 pieces with a wet knife.

  6. 🔪 Adult help: knifeSlice into 8 pieces with a wet knife and serve.

  5. 🔪 Adult help: knifeSlice it into 8 thin, wide pieces.

Japchae 잡채

Glassy sweet-potato noodles with veggies ✨

⏰ 35 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 200 g sweet potato glass noodles (dangmyeon)
  • 1 onion, sliced
  • 1 carrot, sticks
  • 2 cups spinach
  • 5 shiitake mushrooms, sliced
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp sesame oil
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveBoil noodles 6 minutes, drain and rinse.
  2. Snip the noodles a few times with kitchen scissors.
  3. Stir-fry onion, carrot and mushrooms until soft.
  4. Add spinach until it wilts.
  5. Toss in the noodles with soy sauce, sugar and sesame oil.
  6. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve!

Mild Tteokbokki 떡볶이

Chewy rice cakes in a sweet, red sauce 🍡

⏰ 25 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian🥚🧀 Optional egg/cheese
🛒 Ingredients
  • 300 g rice cakes (tteok)
  • 2 cups water with 1 piece kombu (veggie stock)
  • 1 tbsp gochujang (or less for mild!)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1–2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 green onion, 1 cup cabbage
  • Optional: boiled eggs, cheese on top
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Soak the rice cakes in warm water 10 minutes if hard.
  2. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveSimmer kombu in water 5 minutes, then remove it.
  3. Stir in gochujang, soy sauce and sugar.
  4. Add rice cakes and cabbage; simmer 8–10 minutes until saucy.
  5. Add green onions (and eggs or cheese!) and serve.

Hotteok 호떡

Sweet pancakes with a gooey brown-sugar center 🥞

⏰ 1 hr 30 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian🥛 Milk
🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1 tsp instant yeast, 1 tbsp sugar, 1/2 tsp salt
  • 3/4 cup warm milk
  • Filling: 1/2 cup brown sugar, 1 tsp cinnamon, chopped nuts or seeds
  • Oil for the pan
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Mix flour, yeast, sugar, salt and warm milk into a sticky dough.
  2. Cover and let it rise for 1 hour until puffy.
  3. Mix the brown sugar, cinnamon and nuts for the filling.
  4. With oiled hands, flatten a dough ball, add filling and pinch closed.
  5. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveFry in an oiled pan, pressing flat with a spatula, 2–3 min per side.
  6. Careful — the filling is HOT! Let it cool a little first.

Veggie Mandu 야채만두

Plump Korean dumplings packed with tofu, veggies and glass noodles 🥟

⏰ 50 minMedium ⭐⭐🌱 Vegetarian
🛒 Ingredients
  • 24 round dumpling wrappers
  • 1/2 block firm tofu, squeezed dry and crumbled
  • 50 g sweet potato glass noodles (dangmyeon)
  • 2 cups finely chopped cabbage or napa cabbage
  • 4 shiitake mushrooms, chopped
  • 1 carrot, grated · 2 green onions
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil, pinch of salt and pepper
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveBoil the glass noodles 6 minutes, drain, rinse cold and snip them short with scissors.
  2. Mix the tofu, noodles, cabbage, mushrooms, carrot, green onion, soy sauce and sesame oil.
  3. Put a spoonful of filling in a wrapper, wet the edge and fold into a half-moon; press out the air.
  4. Pinch the two corners together for the classic mandu shape (optional!).
  5. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveSteam 8–10 minutes, or pan-fry until golden and then steam with a splash of water under a lid.
  6. Dip in Gyoza Dipping Sauce or Ssamjang.

Veggie Bokkeumbap 야채볶음밥

Speedy veggie fried rice — the best way to use leftover rice 🍚

⏰ 20 minEasy ⭐🌱 Vegetarian🥚 Optional egg
🛒 Ingredients
  • 3 cups cooked rice (day-old is best)
  • 1 carrot, diced
  • 1/2 zucchini, diced
  • 1/2 cup corn + 1/2 cup peas
  • 2 green onions
  • 1 tbsp oil, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sesame oil
  • Optional: 2 eggs, or 1/2 block tofu, crumbled
  • Sesame seeds and roasted seaweed flakes
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. 🔪 Adult help: knifeDice the carrot and zucchini into small, even cubes.
  2. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveHeat oil in a big pan and stir-fry the carrot, zucchini, corn and peas for 3 minutes.
  3. 🔥 Adult help: hot stovePush the veggies aside and scramble the eggs (or tofu) in the empty space.
  4. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveAdd the rice and soy sauce, then stir-fry until every grain is hot and toasty.
  5. Turn off the heat, stir in sesame oil and green onions.
  6. Top with sesame seeds and seaweed flakes and serve.

Spicy Mayo スパイシーマヨ

🌱 Vegetarian🥚 Egg🧊 Fridge: 1 week

Goes with: Avocado Roll, Kappa Maki, Veggie Futomaki, Veggie Kimbap, Veggie Tempura

🛒 Ingredients
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1/2–2 tsp sriracha (start small!)
  • 1 tsp lemon juice
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Stir the mayo and lemon juice together.
  2. Add sriracha a little at a time and taste — make it as mild or spicy as you like.
  3. Check the chili sauce label: pick one with no fish sauce.
✨

Teriyaki Sauce 照り焼きソース

🌱 Vegetarian🧊 Fridge: 2 weeks

Goes with: Yaki Udon, Veggie Onigiri, Veggie Tempura, pan-fried tofu

🛒 Ingredients
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 4 tbsp mirin
  • 2 tbsp sugar
  • 1 tsp cornstarch + 1 tbsp water
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveSimmer soy sauce, mirin and sugar in a small pan for 2 minutes.
  2. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveStir in the cornstarch mix and cook until glossy and thick.
  3. Let it cool — it thickens more as it cools.
🤍

Sesame Dressing ごまドレッシング

🌱 Vegetarian🧊 Fridge: 1 week

Goes with: Cucumber Sunomono, Kappa Maki, Tamago Nigiri, any salad

🛒 Ingredients
  • 3 tbsp toasted sesame seeds (or 2 tbsp tahini)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1–2 tbsp water
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Grind the sesame seeds in a mortar (or use tahini).
  2. Whisk in soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and sesame oil.
  3. Add water until it pours like cream.
🔥

Sweet Soy Sushi Sauce 甘だれ

🌱 Vegetarian🧊 Fridge: 1 month

Goes with: Avocado Roll, Veggie Futomaki, Tamago Nigiri, Kappa Maki, Inari Sushi

🛒 Ingredients
  • 3 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp mirin
  • 3 tbsp sugar
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveHeat soy sauce, mirin and sugar in a small pan, stirring until the sugar melts.
  2. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveSimmer gently 5–8 minutes until syrupy.
  3. Cool, then drizzle on sushi. This is a fish-free "eel sauce" style glaze.

Fish-free Citrus Ponzu 昆布ポン酢

🌱 Vegetarian🧊 Fridge: 1 month

Goes with: Veggie Gyoza, Veggie Tempura, Cucumber Sunomono

🛒 Ingredients
  • 4 tbsp soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp fresh citrus juice (lemon, lime or yuzu)
  • 1 tbsp rice vinegar
  • 1 tbsp mirin
  • 1 small piece kombu (no bonito!)
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. 🔪 Adult help: knifeCut the citrus in half and squeeze out the juice.
  2. Mix soy sauce, juice, vinegar and mirin in a jar and add the kombu.
  3. Chill at least 1 hour (overnight is best), then remove the kombu.

Sushi Pickled Ginger ガリ

🌱 Vegetarian🧊 Fridge: 2 months

Goes with: Kappa Maki, Avocado Roll, Veggie Futomaki, Oshinko Maki, Tamago Nigiri, Inari Sushi

🛒 Ingredients
  • 150 g young ginger
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 cup rice vinegar
  • 3 tbsp sugar
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. 🔪 Adult help: knifePeel the ginger with a spoon, then slice it paper-thin.
  2. Toss the slices with salt, wait 10 minutes and squeeze dry.
  3. 🔥 Adult help: hot stoveHeat the vinegar and sugar until just boiling, then pour the hot brine over the ginger in a clean jar.
  4. Cool and chill for a day — young ginger may turn naturally pink!

Yum Yum Sauce ヤムヤムソース

🌱 Vegetarian🥚 Egg🧊 Fridge: 1 week

Goes with: Veggie Futomaki, Avocado Roll, Kappa Maki, Veggie Bokkeumbap

🛒 Ingredients
  • 4 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp ketchup
  • 1/4 tsp paprika
  • Pinch of garlic powder
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 1–2 tsp water
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Whisk everything except the water together.
  2. Add water a little at a time until it drizzles nicely.
  3. Chill 30 minutes so the flavors blend.

Ssamjang 쌈장

🌱 Vegetarian🧊 Fridge: 1 month

Goes with: Veggie Bibimbap, Veggie Kimbap, Veggie Mandu

🛒 Ingredients
  • 2 tbsp doenjang (Korean soybean paste) — check the label: some contain anchovy, pick a fish-free one
  • 1 tbsp gochujang
  • 1 tbsp sesame oil
  • 1 small clove garlic, grated
  • 1 tsp sesame seeds
👩‍🍳 Steps
  1. Mix doenjang, gochujang and sesame oil until smooth.
  2. Stir in the garlic and sesame seeds.
  3. Taste — add a little honey or water to make it milder.
